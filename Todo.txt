DONT DO THIS:
// Ball Power Ups:
- Fragmentation Ball: fragments the cue ball into multiple smaller balls.
- Heavy/Large Ball: hits number balls harder, moving them faster.
- Explosion Ball: explodes around the cue ball, spreading out the number balls.


ENVIRONMENTAL ASSIST:
- BOOST PAD: applies a velocity to the body in the direction it is pointing.
- WALL: Two sided wall (springy side & absorbant side).
- WINDWILL: SPIN THE BALLS AROUND.
- TELEPORTER:
- (MAYBE) C4: ball hits the C4 block, and explodes, pushing away all balls in a radial area.
